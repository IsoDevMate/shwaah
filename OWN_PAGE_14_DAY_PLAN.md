# 14-DAY OWN-PAGE PLAN (User Zero)
**Goal:** In 14 days your feed can sell you — even if housing/life is not showable.  
**Rule:** Do today’s block only. No “catch up” spirals. Miss a day → do tomorrow’s block, not both.  
**Platforms:** Instagram feed + TikTok (Stories stay native; optional, not required).  
**Shwaah:** You are user zero — every week ends with material in a bank, not only in your head.

---

## Your constraints (built into the plan)

| Constraint | How we handle it |
|------------|------------------|
| Don’t want to show house / environment | Face-optional: screen recordings, voice-over, text-on-color, B-roll of *places you already go* (hackathons, streets, cafés, gyms, events) — never home |
| Calendars die | No 30-day calendar. Only 14 daily blocks. Each block ≤ 25 minutes |
| “I don’t know what to post” | Posts are harvested from life you already live (see Content Bank rules) |
| Portfolio shame | Days 1–3 = cleanup + proof structure; Days 4–14 = evidence you’re the strategist |

---

## Brand DNA for YOU (lock this — 5 lines)

Use this on every post. Do not invent a new persona mid-week.

1. **Who you are:** Nairobi builder in the content/brand trenches — SMM scars, now shipping Shwaah.
2. **What you’re known for:** Turning messy creator/client chaos into a clear presence pipeline (capture → ship → direction).
3. **Tone:** Direct, honest, slightly raw, no guru flex, no fake “10k creators” energy.
4. **Never say:** Fake stats, fear grades, “AI will replace you,” copy-paste hustle porn.
5. **Visual vibe:** Consistent thumbnails — same text style, same 2–3 colors, same framing. Grid must look intentional in 3 seconds.

**Voice test:** If it could be any AI LinkedIn bro, rewrite until it sounds like you confessing to a friend at 11pm.

---

## Content Bank rules (how you never run dry)

Every day, dump **one** of these into Notes / WhatsApp-to-self / Shwaah inspiration bookmark:

- Something you saw a client / brand do wrong or right (no names if sensitive)
- One sentence from a hackathon, community, or conversation
- A Story you already posted that got replies
- A problem you hit building Shwaah (ship-in-public)
- A question a business owner asked you “to town”

**Ban:** “What should I post?” as a starting question. Start from the bank.

## Days 1–3: Mirror + cleanup (portfolio becomes sellable)

### Day 1 — Presence Check (25 min)
1. Open your IG + TikTok as a **stranger** (logged out or another phone).
2. Write 5 bullets (honest):
   - Silent how many days on feed?
   - Do thumbnails match?
   - Would you hire this person?
   - Stories vs feed gap?
   - What do you think you sell in 3 seconds?
3. If Shwaah Health Score / Scout works for you, run it and paste the output under your bullets.
4. **Output artifact:** a note titled `Presence Check — [date]`.

### Day 2 — Grid surgery (25 min)
1. Archive anything that breaks the vibe (random memes, old eras, AI-slop look, off-brand).
2. Keep / pin **3 strongest posts** that prove skill (client work, strategy insight, before/after, process).
3. Decide **next 9 posts theme order** (write titles only):
   - 3 × Proof (client/process scars)
   - 3 × Point of view (presence pipeline / raw shipping)
   - 3 × Product-in-public (building Shwaah without fake numbers)
4. **Do not film today.** Cleanup only.

### Day 3 — Thumbnail + bio system (25 min)
1. **Bio rewrite (use this structure):**  
   `I help [SMMs / local brands] go from “I don’t know what to post” → posts that actually ship.`  
   `Building Shwaah · Nairobi`  
   `↓ [WhatsApp / Calendly / email — one CTA]`
2. Profile photo: clear face or clean logo — one consistent image across IG + TikTok.
3. Pick **one thumbnail formula** for 14 days: e.g. big text left, you or screen right, same background color. Apply to every new post.
4. Highlight covers (IG): 3 max — e.g. Work / Process / Shwaah. Empty highlights > chaotic ones.

## Days 4–10: Ship proof (low-exposure formats only)

**Format menu (pick from these only):**

| Format | How to shoot without home | Time |
|--------|---------------------------|------|
| A. Talking head outdoors / event | Balcony, street, venue wall, car parked | 10 min |
| B. Screen recording + voice | Phone screen of Notes / Canva / Shwaah / IG drafts | 15 min |
| C. Text carousel | Canva, no face | 20 min |
| D. Voice-note → caption post | Record voice, use as IG Reel audio or transcript carousel | 10 min |
| E. Story → Feed rescue | Repost best Story insight as feed with clearer hook | 10 min |

### Day 4 — Post 1: Portfolio shame (POV)
Hook: *“If your own page looks messy, clients won’t pay you to fix theirs.”*  
Body: Your confession + what you’re fixing this week. Format B or C.

### Day 5 — Post 2: Trainer scar (Proof)
Hook: *“I had to put a revision cap in a client agreement. Here’s why.”*  
Body: Busy client + endless revisions → boundary. No client name. Format A or D.

### Day 6 — Post 3: Capture problem (POV)
Hook: *“Scheduling isn’t the hard part. Having nothing to schedule is.”*  
Body: Presence pipeline in plain words. Format B (screen of empty calendar vs bank idea).

### Day 7 — Bank day (no public post required)
1. Dump 7 raw seeds into the bank (one line each).
2. Turn 3 into draft hooks only.
3. Optional: 3 Stories about today (native). Mark best Story for Day 8 rescue.
4. **If using Shwaah:** save inspiration bookmarks / create drafts — even if publish is manual.

### Day 8 — Post 4: Story → Feed rescue
Take best Story from Day 7 (or last 14 days). Make it a proper feed post with hook + payoff. Format E.

### Day 9 — Post 5: Spy / diagnose (Proof of strategist skill)
Hook: *“I went through a content tool funnel that farmed me. Here’s what to steal vs never copy.”*  
Body: Gabber lessons without naming drama if you prefer — mechanism good, fear/fake persona bad. Format C.

### Day 10 — Post 6: Platform voices (POV)
Hook: *“One caption on TikTok and LinkedIn is how brands look amateur.”*  
Body: DNA vs platform expression in 4 slides or 30s. Format C or B.

## Days 11–14: Close the loop (sellable + product-proof)

### Day 11 — Post 7: 10th-floor / local business (Proof)
Hook: *“If your customers can’t walk into the 10th floor, your feed is the front door.”*  
Body: Who you help + what breaks (busy owner, no capture). Format A or D at a real location (not home).

### Day 12 — Post 8: Building Shwaah in public
Hook: *“I’m building the tool I needed when I was drowning in client apps.”*  
Body: Honest — what works today (publish/scout/health), what you’re not faking. **No fake user counts.** Format B (show real UI if safe) or C.

### Day 13 — Soft sell asset (not a hard pitch)
Create **one** of these and pin or save:
- WhatsApp message script to a business: 6–8 lines, offers a free Presence Check (mirror their page in 5 bullets).
- Or a Notion/Notes one-pager: “What I do in 30 days for a client” (capture → weekly queue → proof).

### Day 14 — Review + queue next week (25 min)
1. Re-do Day 1 stranger test. Write: what changed / still broken.
2. Pick next 5 hooks from the bank (titles only).
3. Batch-film **two** Format B or C pieces for next week if energy allows — or just schedule the hooks.
4. Decision: **Are you ready to approach one business / one DM this week?** If yes, send the Day 13 script once. If no, repeat Days 4–6 style posts one more week — do not rebuild the whole strategy.

---

## Weekly rhythm after Day 14 (so you don’t crash out)

**Sunday 15–20 min only:**
1. Empty phone camera roll / Stories → bank (10 items max)
2. Choose 5 → write hooks
3. Shoot 2–3 low-exposure pieces OR draft carousels
4. Queue / calendar the week
5. Log off

If Sunday fails, **Monday 10 min bank dump** is the fallback — not a new master plan.

---

## Success criteria (honest)

You’re done with this plan when **all three** are true:

1. Stranger test: in 3 seconds they can say what you do.  
2. You have **at least 6 new feed posts** in 14 days that match one thumbnail system.  
3. You can send the Presence Check DM **without shame** — because your page no longer contradicts the offer.

Not success: a perfect brand book, 9 posts/day, or a redesigned Shwaah homepage. Those wait.

## If anxiety spikes (housing / visibility)

Allowed content only that day:
- Text carousel
- Screen recording of notes
- Voice-only Reel with solid color or stock/event B-roll you already have

Forbidden that day: filming inside your living space, explaining your situation on camera, “day in the life at home.”
